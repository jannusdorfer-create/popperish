Popperish

Popperish is a maths channel. We build our own simulation engines, run them millions of times, and show what happens: three-body chaos, asteroid impacts, galaxy collisions, folding paper, board games played to the end.

Watch the full episodes on YouTube. Short versions are posted to TikTok and Instagram with the Popperish publisher, our own tool that posts each finished video to the channel's social accounts.

Contact: the channel's About page.

Popperish is run by Jan Nusdorfer, Slovenia.
