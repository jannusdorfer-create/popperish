Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

Who we are

The Popperish publisher is an internal posting tool operated by Jan Nusdorfer (sole proprietor, Slovenia) for the Popperish channel. It is used by the operator only.

What we collect

When the operator connects the Popperish TikTok account, TikTok gives the tool an access token and the account's basic profile (display name, avatar, open id). The tool stores the token in a private file on the operator's computer so it can post videos. It does not collect anything about viewers, followers or any other TikTok user.

What we do with it

The token is used only to upload Popperish videos to the Popperish account and to read the result of each upload. The tool keeps a local log of which video was posted when.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone. No data is sold, and no third-party analytics or advertising services are used.

Retention and deletion

The token is kept until the account is disconnected, which the operator can do at any time in TikTok's app settings or by deleting the local file. Contact the operator through the channel's About page to ask about any data.

Changes

This policy may change. The date above shows the current version.
