Trip Karl

Karl is a crash-test dummy: 77.7 kg, 1.77 m, no muscles and no reflexes, so he never puts a hand out. In our own physics engine we made him fall 10,000 times in each of three places. Every fall on this page is one of those runs, played back pose by pose. Your browser does no physics at all.

Part 4

Measure better?

If twins split because we did not know his speed well enough, knowing it better should keep them together. We ran pairs of twins at every precision. On the stairs it barely helps until the difference is far smaller than anything you could measure. On the hill it does not help at all.
