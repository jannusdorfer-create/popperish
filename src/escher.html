Simulation · Get lost

Get lost

This floor is paved with seven-sided stones, three meeting at every corner. On a flat floor they would not fit, so this one curves like a saddle everywhere, and the further out you look the more stones there are. The disk below squeezes all of it into a circle: the stones are all the same size, they only look smaller toward the rim. Start at the fountain, walk at random, and see if you ever find it again.

Part 1

Get lost

She starts on the stone with the fountain. Every step she crosses one of its seven edges, picked at random, and keeps going until she is home or too far out to ever come back.

Part 3

Why she cannot find her way back

Count the stones you can reach in exactly so many steps from the fountain. On a flat floor each ring holds four more stones than the last. On the paving each ring holds about two and a half times as many as the one before, so a walker is pulled outward far faster than chance can bring her back.
