Simulation · Slime mold

Grow a railway

A slime mold has no brain and no nerve cells: it is one giant cell. Give it oat flakes and it spreads out, finds them, and thins into a network of veins. Here a swarm of 300,000 simulated slime agents does the same. Each one turns toward the strongest trail ahead of it, steps, and leaves a trail of its own. All of it runs on your graphics card.
