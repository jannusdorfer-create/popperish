Privacy and copyright

Short version: this site collects nothing about you, and the simulations run in your own browser.

Who runs this site

popperish.com is run by Jan Nusdorfer, a sole proprietor in Slovenia, who makes the Popperish channel. For any question about this page, write through Ko-fi or the YouTube channel's About page.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. The fonts, the background video and the video covers are served from this site, so no font or video service sees your visit.

The simulations run on your device. The maps you paint stay in your browser. A share link carries the map inside the link itself, so nothing is stored on our side; whoever you send it to can open it.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel handles the technical data a browser sends with each request (such as the IP address and browser type) to deliver the page and keep the service safe. We do not use that data. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Videos

Each video on this site shows our own cover image until you press play. Only then does your browser load YouTube's player, from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. From that moment the player is YouTube's, and Google's privacy policy applies to it, not this one.

Other sites we link to

The Support button goes to Ko-fi and the video links go to YouTube. Once you are there, Ko-fi's and Google's privacy policies apply, not this one.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. If you think we got something wrong, you can also complain to the Slovenian data protection authority, the Information Commissioner.

Copyright

The videos, simulations, code, visuals and text on popperish.com are © 2026 Popperish (Jan Nusdorfer). All rights reserved.

Images you make with the simulations (the PNG download, or a screenshot) are yours to share anywhere, with a credit to Popperish and a link to popperish.com. Using them commercially, or copying the simulations themselves, needs our written permission.
