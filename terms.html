What the Popperish publisher is

The Popperish publisher is an internal tool that posts videos made by Popperish to the Popperish accounts on TikTok and other platforms. It is operated by Jan Nusdorfer (sole proprietor, Slovenia). It is not offered to the public, and it never posts to any account other than Popperish's own.

Use

Only the operator of Popperish uses the tool. By connecting a TikTok account to it you confirm that you own that account and that you agree to TikTok's own terms. The tool posts only the videos you choose, with the captions you approve, and nothing else.

Content

Every video posted through the tool is made by Popperish. Narration is produced with a synthetic voice and is labelled as AI-generated where the platform provides a label.
